Questions.

This is a studio concept, so the answers describe how Ire is designed to work.

Is Ire a real bank?+

Ire is a studio concept by Daniekeys Studios, built to show how a Lagos money app could look and feel. Nothing on this page is a live financial product.

What is a Jar?+

A pot inside your account for one bill or goal. Money in a Jar stays put until the bill is due, so your spending balance always tells the truth.

How does Ajo work?+

Start an Ajo, set the amount and how often, and Ire collects from everyone and pays out to whoever's turn it is.

Can I pay online in dollars?+

In the concept, yes. Make a virtual dollar card for subscriptions and freeze or delete it any time.
